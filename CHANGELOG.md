# Changelog

All notable updates to **Sammy the Adventurer** are recorded here.

## v0.1.1 — Atmosphere and finale polish

- Added gentle scene fades and settling camera motion.
- Added drifting clouds, river ripples, restaurant steam, and garden foliage movement.
- Enhanced the reunion with an arrival animation, memory lights, stars, and petals.
- Cab sound now fades out as Sammy enters Masaldani.

## v0.1.0 — First playable journey

- Five-mission browser adventure from Sammy's bedroom to the final reunion.
- Touch and keyboard controls, automatic progress saving, and resume support.
- Original stylized locations, interactive story moments, and collectible memories.
- Ambient sound for the riverbank and park, plus a final-scene song.
