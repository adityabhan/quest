1. Core Game Concept

Sammy cannot remember where she wants to be or whom she is searching for. Magical discoveries restore fragments of memory. Each memory points her toward the next place until she finally remembers the person she has been trying to find.

  • Genre: Simple 2D, 16-bit pixel-art adventure.
  • Platform: Mobile-friendly browser game.
  • Controls: Swipe to move, tap to interact, and tap to collect.
  • Tone: Cute, personal, nostalgic, and romantic.
  • Structure: Five missions followed by a short castle-garden finale.
  • Design principle: Story first. Gameplay should support the memories, not distract from them.
Scope rule: Keep every mission short and intuitive. Do not add advanced mechanics or extra systems unless explicitly requested.

2. Characters

Sammy

  • Black hair and brown eyes.
  • Small white handbag that stores collected artifacts.
  • Casual, cute outfit; exact colors are not finalized.
  • Main playable character.
  • Her handbag glows briefly when an artifact is collected.

Your Character

  • Black hair and brown eyes.
  • Plain T-shirt and blue jeans.
  • Small brown fanny pack around the waist.
  • Appears at the final destination: the castle garden.

Both characters should use the same compact 16-bit pixel-art style and proportions. Keep the designs simple and recognizable.

3. Mission Sequence

Every discovery restores a new piece of memory and points Sammy toward the next location. The mission order is fixed.

01

Mission 1 — The First Spark

Location: Masaldani

Story and gameplay

  1. A short cinematic introduces Sammy in her bedroom.
  2. A brief playable interaction lets her get ready and pick up her white handbag.
  3. She uses her phone to book a cab and travels to Masaldani.
  4. She enters the restaurant and orders yellow corn lentil soup.
  5. After finishing the soup, she notices a faint golden glow near a decorative plant.
  6. She discovers The First Spark and places it in her handbag.
  7. The artifact triggers a hazy flashback of the first time she saw someone special.
Memory clue: A vague recollection of birds beside a river leads her to the bird sanctuary near the Yamuna bank.
02

Mission 2 — The Guiding Feather

Location: Bird Sanctuary near the Yamuna bank

Story and gameplay

  1. The memory from The First Spark guides Sammy to the sanctuary.
  2. She explores the riverside, surrounded by birds and sunset colors.
  3. While roaming around, she finds a peacock feather with a faint magical glow.
  4. She collects it and places it in her white handbag.
  5. The feather triggers a memory of watching birds and the sunset with someone special.
Memory clue: The memory leads her to Pind Baluchi.
03

Mission 3 — The Heart of Flavor

Location: Pind Baluchi

Story and gameplay

  1. The peacock feather's memory leads Sammy to Pind Baluchi.
  2. She enters the restaurant, finds a table, and orders a glass of chaas (buttermilk).
  3. The chaas arrives, and she drinks it.
  4. Only after she finishes the drink, she notices a glow and discovers a photograph featuring a Sardar mannequin.
  5. She collects the photograph and puts it in her handbag.
  6. The photograph triggers her next memory.
Memory clue: The photograph's memory points Sammy toward the bowling alley. The exact flashback details remain to be finalized.
04

Mission 4 — The Lucky Pin

Location: Bowling alley — venue name TBD

Story and gameplay

  1. Sammy arrives at the bowling alley after the photograph triggers her memory.
  2. She plays a simple bowling interaction.
  3. The goal is to knock down all the pins.
  4. After all the pins are down, she discovers a special glowing bowling pin.
  5. She collects The Lucky Pin and stores it in her handbag.
  6. The artifact triggers a memory of watching the moon with someone special.
Memory clue: The memory of the moon guides her to Machardutam Park.
05

Mission 5 — The Moon Shard

Location: Machardutam Park

Story and gameplay

  1. The memory from The Lucky Pin leads Sammy to Machardutam Park.
  2. She explores the park at night.
  3. She avoids mosquitoes in a brief, playful interaction.
  4. She finds a clear spot and locates the glowing moon.
  5. The moon brings back her final memory: watching it with you.
  6. Sammy finally remembers whom she has been searching for.
  7. Moonlight reveals a magical trail to her final destination.
Mission outcome: The moon itself leads Sammy onward. No extra artifact mission is needed; the final destination is the castle garden.

4. Finale — The Castle Garden

The castle garden is the final destination, not another playable mission.

  1. Sammy follows the moonlit trail to the garden.
  2. She sees your character waiting beneath the moon.
  3. She recognizes him as the person behind her recovered memories.
  4. She walks toward him.
  5. He reaches out his hand, and she takes it.
  6. They hold hands beneath the moon while the collected artifacts glow softly inside her handbag.
Emotional payoff: Sammy wasn't searching for treasure or a reward. She was trying to remember and find you.

Still to decide: Exact dialogue and whether a short final message appears after they hold hands.

6. Simple Gameplay Rules

Swipe to move

Sammy moves through the scene using simple touch gestures. Movement should be predictable and forgiving.

Tap to interact

Tap relevant objects such as the phone, menu, drink, or bowling interaction.

Tap to collect

Important artifacts glow softly. Tapping collects one and triggers a short memory scene.

Short memory scenes

Each flashback reveals one new piece of the story. Avoid long dialogue and complicated cutscenes.

7. Explicitly Out of Scope

  • Complex platforming or jumping physics.
  • Enemies, combat, health bars, or lives.
  • Complicated puzzles or inventory management.
  • Skill trees, upgrades, or elaborate quests.
  • Multiplayer or backend services.
  • Extra missions beyond the five locations and finale.
  • Complex bowling or mosquito mechanics.

Bowling and mosquito avoidance are the only small challenge-like moments, and both should stay easy.

8. What Remains Before Implementation

  1. Finalize memory transitions: Decide the exact flashback caused by the Mission 3 photograph and the Mission 4 bowling pin.
  2. Settle artifact naming: Confirm whether “The Heart of Flavor” and “The Moon Shard” are collectible names, mission names, or both.
  3. Finalize the ending: Decide the dialogue, recognition moment, and any final-screen message.
  4. Finish visual details: Confirm Sammy's outfit colors and the consistent pixel-art look.
  5. Choose implementation approach: Keep it lightweight and browser-based; finalize technology only when ready to build.
  6. Use this document as the reference: Do not start coding until explicitly requested.
